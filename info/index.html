Data policy

We will not share your data, but we will not deliberately protect it either. This is a spinning game: everything you share or keep in this application should be information you are comfortable with other people knowing – or stealing.

Therefore, never provide information that is too personal. For the same reason we will never ask you to create a password. Logging in happens with a login link sent to your e-mail.

  • Server: your username, e-mail, the personal information you enter (nickname, birthday, gender, weight, FTP, difficulty), your home screen and the sessions you save. Your e-mail is only used to send login links and is never deliberately shared.
  • Public vs. private: new profiles are private. A public profile shows your username, nickname, stats and public sessions to everyone.
  • Profile picture: a picture you upload is stored on the server as a public file named after your username – anyone with the link can see it, even when your profile is private.
  • This browser: settings, local overrides and – when the app runs without the server – your sessions are stored locally in the browser.
  • Cookies: only functional cookies that are necessary for the application: one that remembers which riders are logged in on this device (for one year) and one that remembers who is riding right now. No tracking, no advertising.

History

Frode's Bike Trainer is an open source, free project. It started as a shared dream between person X and person Y: making indoor cycling fun by turning the bike into a game controller.
